---
name: Session-change policy boundaries
description: Owner-approval boundaries for financial treatments and attendee communications after schedule changes
---

Do not infer permission to refund, cancel, reissue or transfer purchased tickets from an administrative schedule change. Do not infer permission to deliver held attendee notices or invent session-only referral eligibility.

**Why:** The owner explicitly separated conservative admission preservation/pending-date holds from financial adjustments, attendee consent, notice release and new referral economics. Existing commercial identity and purchased rights must survive later operational edits.

**How to apply:** Keep these decisions explicit in future session-management and notification work. Obtain owner policy before adding an unsupported ticket treatment, releasing notices or introducing session-specific earning/link rules.

Normal session administration must remain an individual-date workflow. Bulk/default operations belong in a separate advanced tool; commerce history alone must not introduce postponement requirements into ordinary content, artwork, host or sponsor saves.

**Why:** The owner rejected the bulk mode-selector grid as the default Wine Down editing experience and explicitly separated protected schedule changes from harmless date-specific content updates.

**How to apply:** Preserve effective-value preview and plain series-detail resets when extending the editor. Trigger schedule warnings only for actual schedule changes, and do not let a reverted tentative schedule change contaminate an unrelated save.
