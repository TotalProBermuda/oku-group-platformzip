# Data-Subject Personal Data Breach Notification Email — Template (EN)

> **[COUNSEL REVIEW PENDING — DO NOT SEND AS-IS]**
>
> This template is an engineering draft prepared so the on-call team
> has a starting point. Panama counsel must review and approve the
> final wording before any email is sent to a data subject. The DPO
> is the only person authorised to remove this banner, and only after
> written counsel approval. ES and PT translations must land before
> first non-English send.

> **This is an engineering operational artefact, not legal advice.**

---

**From:** OKÜ Group — Data Protection Office `<okupanama@gmail.com>`
**Reply-to:** `<okupanama@gmail.com>`
**Subject:** Important notice about your OKÜ account — security
incident on `<YYYY-MM-DD>`
**Send window:** EN, ES, PT translations dispatched in the same
window.

Hello `<First name>`,

We are writing to let you know about a security incident that
affected information you have shared with OKÜ. We are sorry this
happened, and we want to give you the facts plainly.

**What happened.** `<One short paragraph in plain language: what
happened, when it happened, when we became aware. Avoid jargon.
Avoid promising what we don't yet know.>`

**What information of yours was involved.** `<Concrete list — e.g.
"your name and email address", "the last four digits of your bank
account", "your ticket purchase history". Be specific. Say
explicitly what was NOT involved if it is reasonable to do so —
for example: "Your password was not exposed", "Your full bank
account number was not exposed", "Your payment card number is never
stored by OKÜ — Cybersource handles card data directly".>`

**What we have done.** `<Containment + remediation, in plain
language. Mention that we notified ANTAI, the Panamanian data
protection authority, where applicable.>`

**What you can do.** `<Practical steps relevant to the categories
above. Examples — only include those that apply:>`
- _If credentials were involved:_ Change your OKÜ password and any
  password you reuse elsewhere; turn on two-factor where available.
- _If bank info was involved:_ Watch your Banesco account for
  unfamiliar activity and report anything unusual to Banesco.
- _If contact info was involved:_ Be cautious of unexpected emails,
  calls, or messages claiming to be from OKÜ — we will never ask for
  your password.

**Who to contact.** Our Data Protection Officer is `<DPO name>`,
reachable at `<dpo email>` or `<phone>`. You can also contact ANTAI
(Autoridad Nacional de Transparencia y Acceso a la Información), the
Panamanian data protection authority, directly.

**Internal reference.** `INC-YYYY-NNN` — please include this
reference if you write to us about this incident.

Thank you for trusting us with your information. We take that trust
seriously, and we will keep you updated as our investigation
concludes.

— The OKÜ Data Protection Office

## Operational notes for the sender (NOT included in the email)

- Send via the user's account email of record. Do not send to a
  different address even if requested informally — counsel must
  approve any address change.
- Do not include attachments. Do not include links beyond the
  standard OKÜ footer.
- If the incident involves email-system compromise itself, counsel
  decides on an alternative channel (in-app banner, postal, phone).
- Bounces are recorded in the register row as
  `attempted — undeliverable` with the bounce reason.
- ES and PT versions must be the counsel-approved translations from
  `data-subject-notification-email.es.md` and `…pt.md`, not
  machine-translated on the fly.
