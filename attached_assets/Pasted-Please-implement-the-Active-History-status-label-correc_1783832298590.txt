Do NOT treat SEATED as “Closed” or “Completed” for referrer-facing language.

For all pure referrer surfaces, use the same governed protocol:

1. Active / Upcoming
- Non-terminal referral with service date today or future.
- Future reservations must appear immediately after QR/link booking.
- Label: Active or Upcoming.

2. In Service
- ARRIVED or SEATED.
- These are live operational states, not completed states.
- Label: In Service / Seated.

3. Past — Awaiting Close
- Service date is in the past in America/Panama time, but reservation status is still non-terminal, e.g. CONFIRMED or PENDING.
- Do not call this completed.
- This means the restaurant/host/POS has not finalized the outcome yet.

4. Completed / Closed
- Only use Completed/Closed when the reservation actually reaches a terminal completed/closed status, or when POS/check-close evidence confirms the visit closed.
- Completed must never mean “old reservation.”

5. Lost
- CANCELLED or NO_SHOW.
- Keep this separate from Completed.

6. Revenue / payout language
- If check/POS revenue is not closed yet, show something like “Awaiting close” or “Pending revenue evidence.”
- If revenue is verified, show verified spend / accrued commission.
- Do not show paid/unpaid unless it comes from the payout ledger.
