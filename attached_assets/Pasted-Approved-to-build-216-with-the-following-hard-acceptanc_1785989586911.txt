lastTestStatus=null
lastTestedAt=null
lastTestMessage=null

5. Source of truth
After the test settles, card badge, inline Test Result, and global readiness banner must all reflect DB-backed fields:
lastTestStatus
lastTestedAt
lastTestMessage

Transient result.ok can only be used during the immediate request lifecycle, never as the settled readiness truth.

- lastTestStatus="passed", recent lastTestedAt => no blocker
