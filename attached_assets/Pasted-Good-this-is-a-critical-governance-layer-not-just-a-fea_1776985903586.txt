These must be enforced in backend logic, not just UI.

Rule 1: Ledger Separation

All co-host/referrer commissions are tracked in a Partner-Owned Ledger, not the platform ledger.

Meaning:
Platform tracks:
total ticket sales
platform fees
net payable to partner
Partner tracks:
co-host commissions
referral payouts
internal distribution
System implication:
Commission data is informational + reportable, NOT payable by platform
No “payout” button for co-hosts exists in platform
Rule 2: Platform Liability Boundary

The Super Admin is only responsible for payouts to the Partner — not to co-hosts or referrers.

Enforce:
No direct payment flows to co-hosts
No split payout logic (Stripe Connect style) in MVP
No financial obligations recorded against platform for co-host commissions
Rule 3: No Cash Sales — Ever

All tickets and bookings must be processed digitally. Cash is strictly prohibited across all users except Super Admin.

Enforce at system level:
Remove / disable any “manual payment” or “cash” options
API must reject:
payment_method = "cash"

Super Admin ONLY can log manual/cash entries (for reconciliation purposes)

Any attempt to:

bypass payment system
