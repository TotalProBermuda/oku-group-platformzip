5. Desired product logic
For all pure referrers, the language should be proof/referral oriented, not restaurant-ops oriented.

Recommended grouping:
- Active / Upcoming:
  future or current referred reservations that have not reached a terminal outcome
- In Service / Visited:
  arrived/seated if visible and relevant
- History:
  completed, cancelled, no-show, expired service-window, or past reservations
- Pending Close:
  guest visited but check/revenue evidence not closed yet
- Verified Revenue:
  check closed / ticket paid / revenue evidence exists
- Awaiting Payout Ledger:
  commission accrued but not canonically paid

Please recommend corrected labels for streetside and all pure referrer surfaces.

6. Acceptance criteria
- No Confirmed row should appear under “Completed Today.”
- Future-dated QR reservations appear immediately and stay visible until service window or terminal outcome.
- Past non-terminal reservations should be labeled “Past” or “Expired,” not “Completed.”
- “Completed” should mean actual completed/closed state, not simply old.
- All pure referrer surfaces should use consistent Active/History logic from the governed source.
- Labels should be EN/ES/PT i18n-safe.
